# Battery Care, Charging and Storage Guide

Your battery is the most valuable part of your e-bike. With good habits, a Nimbus battery keeps most of its capacity for many years.

## Charging

- Use only the original Nimbus charger. Third-party chargers can damage the battery management system (BMS) and void the warranty.
- Charge indoors at room temperature, between 10 °C and 30 °C.
- It is fine to charge after every ride. You do not need to fully drain the battery first; lithium-ion batteries have no "memory effect".
- A full charge takes 4 to 6 hours depending on battery size.

## Lifespan

A Nimbus battery is rated for 800 to 1,000 full charge cycles before it drops to 70% of its original capacity. For a typical commuter this is five to seven years of riding. Range slowly decreases as the battery ages; this is normal.

## Storage and winter

If you will not ride for more than a month, store the battery at 40% to 60% charge in a cool, dry place. Check the charge every two months and top it up if it falls below 30%. Never store a battery completely empty: deep discharge can permanently damage the cells.

In cold weather range can drop by 20% to 30%. Keep the battery indoors and insert it just before your ride.

## Safety

Do not charge a battery that has been dropped, dented or exposed to water immersion. Contact support if the battery case is swollen, smells unusual or becomes hot while idle.

# Maintenance Schedule and Tips

Regular maintenance keeps your Nimbus safe, quiet and efficient. Most tasks take only a few minutes.

## Before every ride

- Check tire pressure. Recommended pressure is printed on the tire sidewall; for the Stratus this is 3.5 to 5 bar.
- Squeeze both brake levers to make sure the brakes feel firm.
- Check the battery is locked in place.

## Every 500 km

- Clean and lubricate the chain. A dry, noisy chain wastes energy and reduces range.
- Check brake pads for wear. Replace them when less than 1 mm of pad material remains.
- Check that bolts on the stem, handlebar and racks are tight.

## The 1,000 km service

After the first 1,000 km, bring your bike to a certified partner shop for the free 1,000 km service. New cables stretch and spokes settle during the first weeks of riding; the mechanic re-tensions spokes, adjusts gears and brakes, and installs the latest firmware.

## Cleaning

Clean your bike with a damp cloth or a low-pressure hose. Never use a pressure washer: high-pressure water can get past the seals into the motor, the display connector and the battery contacts. Remove the battery before cleaning and let the bike dry before charging.

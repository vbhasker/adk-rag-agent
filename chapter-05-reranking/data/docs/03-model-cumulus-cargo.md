# Nimbus Cumulus Cargo Bike – Specifications

The Cumulus is a long-tail cargo e-bike built to replace a second car. Families use it for school runs, and small businesses use it for local deliveries.

## Carrying capacity

- Maximum total payload: 200 kg (rider plus cargo)
- Rear rack capacity: 80 kg
- Fits up to two child seats on the rear deck, or one child seat plus panniers
- Optional accessories: running boards, a rain canopy and a padded passenger bench

## Motor and battery

- Motor: mid-drive 250 W motor with 85 Nm torque for loaded hill starts
- Battery: 750 Wh main battery
- Dual-battery option: add a second 750 Wh battery for a total of 1,500 Wh
- Range: 50 to 90 km with one battery, up to 170 km with the dual-battery option

## Safety features

The Cumulus has four-piston hydraulic disc brakes, a double-leg kickstand that locks in place for loading children, and wide 20-inch tires for stability. The walk-assist mode helps you push the loaded bike up ramps at 6 km/h.

## Price

The Nimbus Cumulus costs 4,299 USD. The second battery costs 849 USD.

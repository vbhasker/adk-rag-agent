# Returns, Refunds and Shipping

## 30-day test-ride returns

You can return any Nimbus bike within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. If the bike has been ridden more than 50 km, a 10% restocking fee applies to cover inspection and refurbishment. Accessories can be returned unused within 30 days.

To start a return, contact support to receive a return authorisation number (RMA). We send you a prepaid shipping label and a box. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days after we receive the bike.

## Shipping times

- Continental United States: 3 to 7 business days
- Canada: 5 to 10 business days
- European Union: 4 to 8 business days from our Rotterdam warehouse

Bikes ship 90% assembled. You only need to attach the handlebar, pedals and front wheel, which takes about 20 minutes with the included tools.

## Shipping batteries separately

Spare lithium-ion batteries are classified as dangerous goods (UN3480) and must ship by ground transport. They cannot be sent by air freight, so spare batteries are not available for delivery to Hawaii, Alaska or islands without ground or sea freight access.

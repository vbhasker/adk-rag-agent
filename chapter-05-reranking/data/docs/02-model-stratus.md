# Nimbus Stratus City Commuter – Specifications

The Stratus is designed for people who ride to work every day. It is light enough to carry up a flight of stairs and fast enough to keep pace with city traffic.

## Motor and battery

- Motor: 250 W rear hub motor, 45 Nm torque
- Battery: 504 Wh removable lithium-ion battery (36 V, 14 Ah), integrated in the down tube
- Range: 60 to 100 km per charge depending on assist level, rider weight, terrain and temperature
- Charging time: about 4.5 hours from empty to full with the included 4 A charger

## Frame and components

- Frame: 6061 aluminium, available in sizes S, M and L
- Weight: 21 kg including battery
- Brakes: hydraulic disc brakes, 180 mm rotors
- Drivetrain: 8-speed Shimano with a chain guard to keep trousers clean
- Tires: 700x40c puncture-resistant with reflective sidewalls
- Lights: integrated front and rear LED lights powered by the main battery

## Assist levels

The Stratus has five pedal-assist levels: Eco, Tour, Sport, Turbo and Walk. Eco gives the longest range. Turbo gives the strongest support on hills but can reduce range to around 60 km.

## Price

The Nimbus Stratus costs 2,499 USD in the United States and 2,399 EUR in the European Union.

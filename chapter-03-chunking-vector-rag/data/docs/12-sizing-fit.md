# Choosing the Right Frame Size

A bike that fits well is more comfortable, more efficient and safer. Use your height as a starting point, then fine-tune with the saddle and handlebar.

## Frame sizes by rider height

- **Size S:** riders from 150 cm to 165 cm (4'11" to 5'5")
- **Size M:** riders from 165 cm to 180 cm (5'5" to 5'11")
- **Size L:** riders from 180 cm to 195 cm (5'11" to 6'5")

If you are between two sizes, choose the smaller size for a more upright, relaxed position or the larger size for a sportier, stretched-out position.

## One size fits most

The Cirrus folding bike and the Cumulus cargo bike come in a single size that fits riders from 155 cm to 195 cm thanks to a telescopic seat post and adjustable stem.

## Fine-tuning your fit

Set the saddle height so your leg is almost straight, with a slight bend at the knee, when the pedal is at its lowest point. Partner shops offer a free 15-minute fitting session when you pick up your bike.

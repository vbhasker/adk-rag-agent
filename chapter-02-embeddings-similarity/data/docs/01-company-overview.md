# About Nimbus Bikes and the 2026 Lineup

Nimbus Bikes is an electric bicycle company founded in 2019 in Portland, Oregon. We design e-bikes for everyday riders: commuters, parents hauling kids, and weekend trail explorers. Every Nimbus bike is assembled in our Portland workshop and ships with a pre-configured motor, battery and the Nimbus Ride app.

## The 2026 lineup

- **Nimbus Stratus** – our best-selling city commuter. Lightweight, fast, and made for daily rides to work.
- **Nimbus Cirrus** – a folding e-bike that fits under a desk or in a car trunk. Popular with train commuters and apartment dwellers.
- **Nimbus Cumulus** – a long-tail cargo e-bike that can carry two children or a full load of groceries.
- **Nimbus Trail** – a full-suspension electric mountain bike (eMTB) with a mid-drive motor for steep climbs.

## Our promise

We believe owning an e-bike should feel as easy as owning a regular bicycle. That is why every bike includes a lifetime frame warranty, free firmware updates, and access to our support team seven days a week via chat, email (help@nimbusbikes.example) or phone.

## Where to buy

Nimbus bikes are sold directly on our website and through more than 120 certified partner shops in the United States, Canada and the European Union. Certified partner shops can also perform warranty repairs and the 1,000 km service.

# E-Bike Safety and Legal Speed Classes

E-bike rules depend on where you ride. Nimbus bikes ship pre-configured for the legal limits of the country they are sold in.

## European Union

In the EU, a pedelec (EPAC) may have a maximum continuous motor power of 250 W, and motor assistance must stop at 25 km/h. A throttle is only allowed as a walk-assist up to 6 km/h. Bikes that exceed these limits are classified as mopeds and need registration, insurance and a licence.

## United States

Most US states use a three-class system:

- **Class 1:** pedal-assist only, assistance stops at 20 mph (32 km/h).
- **Class 2:** throttle allowed, motor stops at 20 mph.
- **Class 3:** pedal-assist only, assistance stops at 28 mph (45 km/h); helmets are required in most states and minimum rider age is often 16.

The Stratus and Cirrus are sold in the US as Class 1. The Cumulus is Class 2 because it has a throttle for loaded starts. The Trail is Class 1.

## Riding safely

Always wear a helmet, use your lights in low visibility, and remember that drivers often underestimate the speed of an e-bike. Modifying your bike to go faster than the legal limit is illegal in many places and voids your warranty.

# Nimbus Warranty Policy

Every Nimbus e-bike purchased from nimbusbikes.example or a certified partner shop is covered by the warranty below. The warranty applies to the original owner and requires registration in the Nimbus Ride app or on our website within 60 days of purchase.

## Coverage periods

- **Frame and fork:** lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects.
- **Motor, controller, display and wiring:** 2 years.
- **Battery:** 2 years or 500 full charge cycles, whichever comes first. A battery is considered defective if it holds less than 70% of its original capacity within this period.
- **Components** such as brakes, gears, tires and saddles: 1 year against manufacturing defects.

## What is not covered

- Normal wear and tear: brake pads, tires, chains, cassettes and grips.
- Damage from crashes, misuse, improper storage or water immersion.
- Damage from third-party chargers, batteries or unauthorised motor tuning ("speed chips" or "derestriction kits").
- Commercial rental use, unless the bike was sold under a Nimbus fleet agreement.

## How to make a claim

Open a ticket in the Nimbus Ride app or email help@nimbusbikes.example with your serial number, proof of purchase and photos or a video of the issue. Repairs are done by a certified partner shop or at our Portland workshop. We cover shipping costs for approved warranty claims.

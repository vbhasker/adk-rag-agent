# Troubleshooting – My E-Bike Will Not Turn On

If the display stays dark when you press the power button, work through these steps in order. Most cases are solved in a few minutes without tools.

## Step 1 – Check the battery

Make sure the battery is charged. Press the button on the side of the battery: the LEDs show the state of charge. If no LEDs light up, charge the battery for at least 30 minutes and try again.

## Step 2 – Check the battery is seated and locked

A battery that is not fully clicked into the frame will not make contact. Remove the battery, check the contacts for dirt, then slide it back in until it clicks and lock it with the key.

## Step 3 – Wake up the battery

After long storage the battery goes into deep-sleep mode to protect itself. Connect the charger for 10 seconds to wake it up.

## Step 4 – Check the display connection

Check the cable between the display and the handlebar. A loose display connector is the most common reason for a bike that has power at the battery but shows nothing on screen.

## Still dead?

If the battery LEDs light up but the display stays off, contact support. If the battery LEDs do not light up even after charging, the battery may need a replacement under warranty.

# Nimbus Ride App, Firmware Updates and Anti-Theft

The Nimbus Ride app for iOS and Android connects to your bike over Bluetooth Low Energy. It shows battery level, remaining range, ride history, and lets you customise assist levels.

## Pairing your bike

1. Turn on the bike and open the Nimbus Ride app.
2. Tap "Add bike" and hold your phone near the display.
3. Enter the six-digit pairing code shown on the display.

If pairing fails, turn Bluetooth off and on again on your phone and make sure no other phone is connected to the bike. Only one phone can be connected at a time.

## Firmware updates

Nimbus releases over-the-air (OTA) firmware updates for the motor controller and display. Updates install through the app and take about 10 minutes. Keep the bike switched on and the battery above 30% during the update. Firmware 4.2.1 (March 2026) improved range estimates in cold weather and added a hill-hold feature for the Cumulus.

## Anti-theft and GPS

Stratus, Cumulus and Trail models include a built-in GPS tracker with a 3-year connectivity plan. If your bike moves while locked, the app sends an alert and the motor is disabled until you unlock it with your phone. You can share the live location with the police from the app.
